This project is playable through Itch.io: https://art1factual.itch.io/propulsify

You can run the game in the browser through any and all platforms.

*Made In Unity*

Built over a week through Hack Club's Terra Prep Week 3. 
Utilizes Unity-Assets along with tutorials for help.

Yet the work was implemented and coded by Artifactual.

**How to play:**

Simply place different blocks in the path of the drone, then click play and watch the physics-accurate logic play out.
Can you stop the bomb-leading rogue drone?

In the Starting Blueprint Scene, click the down arrow to switch your pipe assets.
And rotate with R to align and orientate your pipes for constructions.
Then click the circle to commence the build and flight program.\
